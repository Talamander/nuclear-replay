DOWNLOADED ON REQUEST, NOT INCLUDED
-----------------------------------

FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe)
  The video encoder that clips and renders use. It is not part of the
  release zip. Your computer downloads it from its publisher only when you
  ask: Yes in the replay viewer's prompt, "nr install" or
  "nr ffmpeg install". The exact build (version, URL, size, SHA-256) is
  pinned in shared/Ffmpeg/FfmpegPackage.cs; the download is checked
  against that pin before it is used.
  Only ffmpeg.exe and its license are kept, as
  <game>/NuclearReplay/tools/ffmpeg/ffmpeg.exe and LICENSE.txt.
  ffmpeg is a separate program that the game starts; it is not linked into
  Nuclear Replay. License: GNU General Public License v3 (the build
  includes GPL libraries such as x264); the full text comes with the
  download. Windows build by gyan.dev (GyanD/codexffmpeg on GitHub).
  You can use your own ffmpeg.exe instead: set FfmpegPath in
  BepInEx/config/talamander.nuclearreplay.player.cfg.
